Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2 November 2026

Ifographic is a private tool used only by the channel owner. It connects to the owner's own YouTube, Google Drive, Instagram and TikTok accounts to upload videos the owner has made.

  • Data accessed: only what is needed to upload videos and read basic account details (account name and ID) for the owner's own accounts.
  • Data stored: access tokens for those accounts, kept privately and used only to publish videos.
  • Data shared: none. No data is sold or shared with third parties.
  • Other users: the tool does not collect data from viewers or any other users.

Access can be revoked at any time from the relevant platform's security settings. Use of Google data follows the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.
